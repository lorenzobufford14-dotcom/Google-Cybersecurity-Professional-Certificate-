## 1. Course Overview & Core Objectives
This module established the foundational principles of information security, focusing on business risk management, core defensive frameworks, primary security domains, and the historical context of cyber threats.

---

## 2. Key Frameworks & Concepts Mastered

### Core Security Principles**
* **The CIA Triad:** Evaluated how Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability balance business operations against technical risks.
* **Security vs. Privacy:** Distinguished between safeguarding system infrastructure/data from unauthorized access (Security) and managing personal data handling, compliance, and user consent (Privacy).

### Security Operations & Roles**
* **SOC Operations:** Explored the operational structure of Security Operations Centers, entry-level analyst workflows, and daily threat monitoring responsibilities.
* **8 CISSP Domains:** Analyzed core domains including Asset Security, Security Architecture & Engineering, Communication & Network Security, and Identity & Access Management (IAM).

### Frameworks & Compliance**
* **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF):** Studied the core functions: **Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover**.
* **Historical Threats & Attack Vectors:** Analyzed real-world security incidents and common attack vectors (phishing, social engineering, malware).

---

## 3. Professional Application
Understood how foundational security policies, risk assessment models, and standard operational frameworks allow entry-level cybersecurity analysts to align technical controls with organizational security goals.
